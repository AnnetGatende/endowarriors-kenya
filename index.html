Kenyan MRI survival notes

Before you start
  • Ask whether an angled prism/mirror is available if it helps you feel oriented.
  • Ask where the emergency call button/squeeze bulb is and how to use it.
  • Tell the radiographer about claustrophobia, pain, implants and medications.
What may happen
  • Some scans use contrast; staff should explain why it is needed and check relevant risks.
  • Buscopan may be used in some pelvic imaging protocols to reduce bowel movement; follow the radiology team's instructions.
  • After laparoscopy, shoulder-tip pain can occur from gas irritating the diaphragm/phrenic nerve; ask your surgical team how to manage it.

Food & comfort

Kenyan foods, without miracle claims.

Food can support overall nutrition, but no food is proven to remove endometriosis lesions. Use this guide to build balanced meals and identify your own tolerances.

Limit if they trigger you

Ultra-processed/refined foods, alcohol, or other foods that consistently worsen your symptoms can be discussed with a clinician or dietitian.

Golden Ginger & Turmeric Elixir

Warm water + fresh ginger + turmeric. Treat it as a comfort drink, not a substitute for prescribed treatment.

Simple balanced plate

Greens + a protein such as fish/beans + a filling carbohydrate + fruit, adjusted to your tolerance and budget.

Workplace note

Kenyan employment law contains sick-leave provisions, but the exact entitlement depends on the applicable law, contract and circumstances. Ask HR and check the current Employment Act/official guidance rather than relying on a generic template.

Dear HR Team, I am managing a diagnosed medical condition that may require periodic medical appointments and, during acute episodes, medically supported rest. I will provide appropriate documentation and communicate as early as possible. I am requesting a confidential conversation about reasonable scheduling and leave arrangements available under my employment terms and applicable Kenyan law. Thank you for handling this information sensitively.
